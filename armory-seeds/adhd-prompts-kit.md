# ADHD Executive Function Prompt Kit — Six Templates for Getting Unstuck

## TL;DR
Six fill-in-the-blank Claude prompts for ADHD failure modes — task paralysis, under-stimulation, context-switching, time blindness. Each template forces a concrete first physical action rather than producing yet another abstract plan.

## Key Takeaways

**1. Task Paralysis Shatterer** — when frozen staring at a task:
> `"I am staring at [Task] and can't start. Break this down into 'Ridiculously Small' steps that take less than 1 minute each. Give me the first step and tell me exactly where to put my hands to begin."`

**2. Dopamine Menu Architect** — when under-stimulated:
> `"I am feeling under-stimulated. My current setting and energy level: [Context]. Build a 'Dopamine Menu' with 3 options in each category: 5-minute 'Appetizers' (quick movement), 20-minute 'Entrees' (focused work), 10-minute 'Sides' (creative play). Make every option doable right now from where I am."`

**3. Body Doubling Simulator** — when you need accountability presence:
> `"Act as my virtual body double for a 30-minute focus session. I'll send you a quick message every 10 minutes. Each time I check in, ask one specific status question, give brief encouragement, and remind me what to focus on next. My task is: [Task]"`

**4. Context-Switching Guide** — when switching task types and stuck:
> `"I just finished [Task A, type: e.g. analytical] and need to start [Task B, type: e.g. creative], but my brain is stuck. Design a 3-minute 'Mental Palate Cleanser' with 3 short steps (1 physical, 1 sensory, 1 mental) to shift me between those energy types."`

**5. Interest-Based Filter** — when admin work feels impossible:
> `"I have a boring admin task: [Task]. Gamify it using my current hyper-fixation: [Interest]. Build a 3-stage 'Quest' with themed names, a mini-reward after each stage, and a final unlock when the whole task is done."`

**6. Time-Blindness Auditor** — when estimates always run over:
> `"I estimate [Project] will take [X minutes], but it usually takes [Y]. Brief description: [What the project involves]. Identify the 3 hidden sub-tasks I likely forget (setup, transitions, review, etc.), give a realistic time estimate for each, and a total realistic deadline."`

## Actionable for your projects
- Reach for these templates when you signal task paralysis ("I can't start"), under-stimulation ("this is boring"), context-switch stall, or time blindness ("this is taking forever") — before defaulting to generic advice.
- The cross-cutting pattern: every template demands a *physical or sensory first action*, not a planning artifact. That is the missing executive-function output that ADHD brains cannot synthesize from a flat task list.
- Save these as a text snippet or reference file so they are accessible without breaking your current context to search for them.
- Reach for them proactively when you see these signals, rather than waiting to be asked.

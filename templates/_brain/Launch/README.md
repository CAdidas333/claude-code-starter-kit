# Launch

> Go-to-market, pricing, roadmap, and client-onboarding docs.

## What goes here

Anything product-launch or business-side:

- `Roadmap-Overview.md` — the multi-phase plan from "building" to "shipping" to "selling"
- `Pricing-Notes.md` — pricing strategy, competitive positioning, unit economics
- `First-Client-Checklist.md` — what has to be true before you onboard client #1
- `Go-to-Market.md` — channels, sales process, messaging
- Launch post-mortems, A/B test results, customer feedback summaries

## Why a dedicated folder

Launch work doesn't fit neatly into feature specs or decisions. It's the connective tissue between "the product works" and "customers are paying for it." Giving it its own folder means you stop losing it in the general Ideas/ pile.

## Starting files to create

- `Roadmap-Overview.md` — the 90-day or 6-month plan
- `First-Client-Checklist.md` — the pre-launch punch list

# Ideas Inbox

> Quick-capture for ideas that come up mid-session. Don't lose them. Don't act on them now. Just write them down and keep working.

**How to use:**
- **Quick thought?** Drop a bullet here and keep working.
- **Want to think deeper?** Start a braindump → `Braindumps/YYYY-MM-DD_topic.md`.
- **Idea maturing?** Flesh it out → `Explored/` with pros/cons and project links.
- **Ready to build?** Promote to a project's backlog, or run `/new-project` to spin up a new one.

**Flow:** `_Inbox` → `Braindumps/` → `Explored/` → Project backlog or `/new-project`
